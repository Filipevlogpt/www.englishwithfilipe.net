# English with Filipe — 3-Language Bank V6.4

The bank now contains **500 terms for each CEFR level A1, A2, B1, B2 and C1**, plus the original all-level grammar labels. English entries are unique across the complete bank.

The bank combines high-frequency vocabulary already present in English with Filipe and additional everyday expressions built from that core vocabulary. It is intended for practical daily use in English, Brazilian Portuguese and Romanian rather than as a claim of a single exact frequency ranking across the three languages.

The selection approach was checked against public frequency-oriented reference lists for Brazilian Portuguese, English and Romanian, including:
- Wiktionary's Brazilian Portuguese frequency list.
- LearnEntry's 1,000 common Romanian words.
- Public English common-word lists.

The platform keeps the three-language content separate from the interface language: changing EN/PT/RO changes the interface, while English learning terms remain visible in English.
