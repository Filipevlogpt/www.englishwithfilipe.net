# 3-Language Bank — V6.5

The bank is organized as 500 unique English terms per CEFR level (A1–C1), with no repeated English term across levels.

The selection was cleaned to remove the previous generated filler patterns and expanded using common vocabulary appearing in the platform test corpus. For external frequency context, the project references public frequency resources for English and multilingual common-word lists, including FrequencyWords and the multilingual frequency-list project. These sources are frequency references, not a single official ranking of everyday Romanian + Brazilian Portuguese + English.

External references:
- https://github.com/oprogramador/most-common-words-by-language
- https://github.com/frekwencja/most-common-words-multilingual
- https://github.com/appsinacup/polyglot-dictionaries

Important: some newly added terms are marked with an em dash when a PT/RO translation was not available in the existing curated bank and should be reviewed by the teacher before being treated as a final translation.
