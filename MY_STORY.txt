Before I became a teacher, I was a learner.

And I know what it feels like to learn English on your own.

My name is Filipe. I'm originally from Brazil, and I've been living in Europe for more than seven years. I'm currently based in Romania. Although I'm new to teaching English professionally, I'm not new to English or international environments.

I've worked with multinational companies such as Booking.com, TAP Airport, Radisson Blu Hotels, and other European companies in Portugal. Those experiences showed me how useful English is for work, travel, communication, and everyday life.

I started learning English by myself in 2016. I didn't have a teacher guiding me every day, so I created my own routine. I would go to my room and study English every day, practicing the different skills I needed.

I didn't just study English. I tried to make English something I actually used.

I wrote sentences. I read them. I spoke them out loud so I could hear myself and notice how I was speaking. I repeated this for hours and hours.

I also kept a notebook with the sentences I was learning. I would write sentences down, review them, and practice them again. After about nine months, I had completed the Duolingo course and could speak English and express myself.

But the biggest change was confidence. That confidence came from lots and lots of practice.
